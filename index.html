Layer 1 · Epistemological Foundation
第一层 · 认识论根基

📡 The Information Isomorphism Law信息同构定律

The framework’s proposed account of scientific knowability
为什么世界可知

The proposed law states that, in objective-data systems exhibiting regime switching, statistical structures can carry information about regime structures. The paper programme treats this as both an ontological claim about structural information and an epistemological claim about how such information may be recovered. Its validity is conditional on the assumptions and model classes developed in the corresponding papers.

该定律的主张是：在存在体制切换的客观数据系统中，统计结构可能承载体制结构的信息。本研究计划将其同时作为关于结构信息的本体论主张，以及关于如何恢复这类信息的认识论主张。其有效性仍受相应论文中所列假设与模型类约束。

Three progressive ontological levels: Existence (regime switching leaves quantifiable traces) → Correspondence (traces systematically correspond to regime attributes) → Precision (encoding is exact on truth-known systems).

三个递进的本体论层次：存在性（体制切换留下可量化痕迹）→ 对应性（痕迹与体制属性存在系统对应）→ 精确性（在真理已知系统上编码精确）。

Key anchor: 2D Ising exact solution — Chow F achieves global unique maximum at exact Tc (F = 118,074); bias strictly zero in analytical sense.
关键锚点：2D Ising 精确解——Chow F 在精确 Tc 处取得全局唯一最大值（F = 118,074）；偏差在解析意义上严格为零。
Layer 2 · Ontology
第二层 · 本体论

📐 The Factor Hierarchy Law因子层级定律

What the world is — regime factors vs execution factors
世界如何组织——规则因子与执行因子

The proposed law classifies factors in the studied multi-factor systems into two roles: regime factors (which determine which set of rules applies) and execution factors (which operate under those rules). It further hypothesizes that crossing a regime boundary can systematically reset execution-factor exposures. The papers specify the model classes and conditions under which these claims are tested.

该定律将所研究的多因子系统中的因子区分为两种角色：规则因子（决定当前适用哪套规则）与执行因子（在相应规则下发挥作用）。论文进一步提出，越过体制边界可能系统性重置执行因子的暴露系数；相关检验受论文声明的模型类与条件约束。

  • Axiom 1 (Factor Bifurcation): Factors naturally divide into regime factors and execution factors.
  • Axiom 2 (Regime Switch): When a regime factor crosses its critical value, the factor weights of the entire system are systematically reset.
  • Axiom 3 (Interaction Effect): The function of a regime factor is not in its direct explanatory power, but in its modulation of execution factors' exposure coefficients.
  • 公理1（因子二分法）：因子天然分为规则因子与执行因子两个层级。
  • 公理2（体制切换）：当规则因子越过临界值时，整个系统的因子权重被系统性重置。
  • 公理3（交互效应）：规则因子的功能不体现在直接解释力上，而体现在它调节执行因子暴露系数的交互效应中。
Selected case studies reported in the author’s papers: finance (China–U.S. spread), astronomy (proxy regime factor), particle physics (color charge), chemistry (period/valence electrons), nuclear physics (N and Z), and statistical physics (temperature). The evidence types and model assumptions differ across areas.
作者论文中的部分案例：金融学（中美利差）、天文学（代理规则因子）、粒子物理（色荷）、化学（周期/价电子）、核物理（N与Z）和统计物理（温度）。不同领域的证据类型与模型假设并不相同。
Layer 3 · Epistemological Standard
第三层 · 认识论标准

⚖️ The Testability Principle可检验性原则

What counts as reliable — beyond falsifiability
怎样才算可靠——超越可证伪性

The Testability Principle proposes a second methodological threshold alongside Popperian falsifiability: a claim about factor importance should be evaluated through a complete set of regime-dependence tests before being treated as reliable. This is the author’s proposed methodological framework, not a standard already adopted across the sciences.

可检验性原则在波普尔的可证伪性之外，提出第二道方法论门槛：关于因子重要性的主张，在被视为可靠结果之前，应接受完整的体制依赖检验。这是作者提出的方法论框架，并非已经被各科学领域普遍采用的标准。
