The Factor Hierarchy System (因子层级体系) is a unified theoretical framework for cross-disciplinary complex systems research. 🧭 Core Laws & Principle核心定律与原则

Law 1 · Ontology
定律一 · 本体论

📐 The Factor Hierarchy Law

📐 因子层级定律

How the world is organized — regime factors vs execution factors
世界如何组织——规则因子与执行因子

The Factor Hierarchy Law states that in any complex system driven by multiple quantifiable factors, factors are naturally divided into two layers: regime factors (which determine which set of rules currently applies) and execution factors (which execute under the given rules). When a regime factor crosses a critical value, the entire system's factor weights are systematically reset.

因子层级定律指出：在任何由多个可量化因子驱动的复杂系统中，因子天然分为两个层级：规则因子（决定当前适用哪套规则）与执行因子（在给定规则下执行）。当规则因子越过临界值时，整个系统的因子权重被系统性重置。

  • Axiom 1 (Factor Bifurcation): Factors naturally divide into regime factors and execution factors.
  • Axiom 2 (Regime Switch): When a regime factor crosses its critical value, the factor weights of the entire system are systematically reset.
  • Axiom 3 (Interaction Effect): The function of a regime factor is not in its direct explanatory power, but in its modulation of execution factors' exposure coefficients.
  • 公理1（因子二分法）：因子天然分为规则因子与执行因子两个层级。
  • 公理2（体制切换）：当规则因子越过临界值时，整个系统的因子权重被系统性重置。
  • 公理3（交互效应）：规则因子的功能不体现在直接解释力上，而体现在它调节执行因子暴露系数的交互效应中。

Law 2 · Epistemological Foundation
定律二 · 认识论根基

📡 The Information Isomorphism Law

📡 信息同构定律

Why the world is knowable — statistical structures carry regime-structure information
为什么世界可知——统计结构承载体制结构信息

In complex systems grounded in objective data and exhibiting regime switching, statistical structures carry information about regime structures. This law is a dual-identity meta-law: as an ontological law, it describes how the world encodes information; as an epistemological meta-law, it answers the fundamental question of how we can know the world.

在以客观数据为基础、存在体制切换的复杂系统中，统计结构承载了体制结构的信息。该定律是双重身份元规律：作为本体论定律，它描述世界的信息编码方式；作为认识论元规律，它回答"我们如何能认识世界"这一根本问题。

Three progressive ontological levels: Existence (regime switching leaves quantifiable traces) → Correspondence (traces systematically correspond to regime attributes) → Precision (encoding is exact on truth-known systems).

三个递进的本体论层次：存在性（体制切换留下可量化痕迹）→ 对应性（痕迹与体制属性存在系统对应）→ 精确性（在真理已知系统上编码精确）。

Principle 3 · Epistemological Standard
原则三 · 认识论标准

⚖️ The Testability Principle

⚖️ 可检验性原则

What counts as reliable — beyond falsifiability
怎样才算可靠——超越可证伪性

After Popper's falsifiability, the Testability Principle establishes the second threshold for science. Falsifiability guards the entrance (distinguishing science from non-science); Testability guards the exit (distinguishing reliable findings from unverified hypotheses). Any claim about factor importance in a multi-factor system must pass complete regime-dependent tests before being accepted as reliable knowledge.

在波普尔的可证伪性之后，可检验性原则为科学确立了第二道门槛。可证伪性守卫入口（区分科学与非科学）；可检验性守卫出口（区分可靠发现与未经验证的假说）。任何关于多因子系统因子重要性的声称，必须通过完整的体制依赖检验，才能被接受为可靠知识。

The principle is operationalized as the Testability Norms — six functional dimensions. Core commitment: functional dimensions are stable; implementation tools are replaceable.

该原则操作化为可检验性规范——六个功能维度。核心承诺：功能维度稳定，实现工具可变。

2026g: Testability Principle and Norms established → 2026r: Meta-law of meta-laws; operating system → 2026z: Technical specification
