My research has established the Factor Hierarchy System (因子层级体系)—a unified theoretical framework for cross-disciplinary complex systems research. The system is known by three names, each focusing on a different component: Factor Hierarchy System (natural science, ontology), Information Isomorphism Epistemology (philosophy, epistemological foundation), and Scientific Discovery Operating System (engineering, methodology). Its minimal core is two laws, one principle, and one norm: the Factor Hierarchy Law (regime vs execution factors, 3 axioms), the Information Isomorphism Law (statistical structures carry information about regime structures), the Testability Principle (beyond falsifiability), and the Testability Norms (6 functional dimensions, stable with replaceable tools). Deployed across six disciplines—finance, astronomy, statistical physics, particle physics, chemistry, and nuclear physics. Documented in 48 preprints (2026), permanently archived on Zenodo with full open data and code, indexed on SSRN.
