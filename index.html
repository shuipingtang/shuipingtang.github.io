Law 1 · Ontology
定律一 · 本体论

📐 The Factor Hierarchy Law

📐 因子层级定律

How the world is organized — regime factors vs execution factors
世界如何组织——规则因子与执行因子

In any complex system driven by multiple quantifiable factors, factors are naturally divided into two layers: regime factors (which determine which set of rules currently applies) and execution factors (which execute under the given rules). When a regime factor crosses a critical value, the entire system's factor weights are systematically reset.

在任何由多个可量化因子驱动的复杂系统中，因子天然分为两个层级：规则因子（决定当前适用哪套规则）与执行因子（在给定规则下执行）。当规则因子越过临界值时，整个系统的因子权重被系统性重置。

  • Axiom 1 (Factor Bifurcation): Factors naturally divide into regime factors and execution factors.
  • Axiom 2 (Regime Switch): When a regime factor crosses its critical value, the factor weights of the entire system are systematically reset.
  • Axiom 3 (Interaction Effect): The function of a regime factor is not in its direct explanatory power, but in its modulation of execution factors' exposure coefficients.
  • 公理1（因子二分法）：因子天然分为规则因子与执行因子两个层级。
  • 公理2（体制切换）：当规则因子越过临界值时，整个系统的因子权重被系统性重置。
  • 公理3（交互效应）：规则因子的功能不体现在直接解释力上，而体现在它调节执行因子暴露系数的交互效应中。
Mathematical series: Switching Type Classification Theorem · Six-Class Hierarchical Structure Exhaustiveness · Geometric Necessity of Structural Breaks · η Divergence Theorem.
Empirical evidence: Finance (China-U.S. spread), Astronomy (proxy regime factor), Particle Physics (color charge), Chemistry (period/valence electrons), Nuclear Physics (N and Z, η > 10⁶), Statistical Physics (temperature).
数学系列：切换类型分类定理 · 六类层级结构穷尽性 · 结构断裂几何必然性 · η发散定理。
实证证据：金融学（中美利差）、天文学（代理规则因子）、粒子物理（色荷）、化学（周期/价电子）、核物理（N与Z，η > 10⁶）、统计物理（温度）。
Law 2 · Epistemological Foundation
定律二 · 认识论根基

📡 The Information Isomorphism Law

📡 信息同构定律

Why the world is knowable — statistical structures carry regime-structure information
为什么世界可知——统计结构承载体制结构信息

In complex systems grounded in objective data and exhibiting regime switching, statistical structures carry information about regime structures. This law is a dual-identity meta-law: as an ontological law, it describes how the world encodes information; as an epistemological meta-law, it answers how we can know the world.

在以客观数据为基础、存在体制切换的复杂系统中，统计结构承载了体制结构的信息。该定律是双重身份元规律：作为本体论定律，它描述世界的信息编码方式；作为认识论元规律，它回答"我们如何能认识世界"。

Three progressive ontological levels: Existence (regime switching leaves quantifiable traces) → Correspondence (traces systematically correspond to regime attributes) → Precision (encoding is exact on truth-known systems).

三个递进的本体论层次：存在性（体制切换留下可量化痕迹）→ 对应性（痕迹与体制属性存在系统对应）→ 精确性（在真理已知系统上编码精确）。

Mathematical series: L² Sufficiency Theorem (X-dependent convex combination) · Detection resolution–information lower bound duality · No-False-Peak Theorem (permutation exactness) · Locally Weighted Unified Shape Function (one-to-one correspondence).
Key anchor: 2D Ising exact solution — Chow F achieves global unique maximum at exact Tc (F = 118,074); bias strictly zero in analytical sense.
数学系列：L² 充分性定理（X-依赖凸组合）· 检测分辨率–信息下界对偶 · 无假峰定理（置换精确性）· 局部加权统一形状函数（一一对应）。
关键锚点：2D Ising 精确解——Chow F 在精确 Tc 处取得全局唯一最大值（F = 118,074）；偏差在解析意义上严格为零。
Principle 3 · Epistemological Standard
原则三 · 认识论标准

⚖️ The Testability Principle

⚖️ 可检验性原则

What counts as reliable — beyond falsifiability
怎样才算可靠——超越可证伪性

After Popper's falsifiability, the Testability Principle establishes the second threshold for science. Falsifiability guards the entrance (science vs non-science); Testability guards the exit (reliable findings vs unverified hypotheses). Any claim about factor importance must pass complete regime-dependent tests.

在波普尔的可证伪性之后，可检验性原则为科学确立了第二道门槛。可证伪性守卫入口（区分科学与非科学）；可检验性守卫出口（区分可靠发现与未经验证的假说）。任何关于因子重要性的声称，必须通过完整的体制依赖检验。
