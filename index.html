My research has established a Scientific Discovery Operating System—a proceduralized, cross-disciplinary, impersonal framework for extracting reliable laws from objective data. Comprises four components: the Information Isomorphism Law (epistemological foundation), the Factor Hierarchy Law (ontology: regime vs execution factors, 3 axioms), the Testability Principle (epistemological standard: beyond falsifiability), and the Testability Norms (methodology: 6 functional dimensions, stable with replaceable tools). Deployed across six disciplines—finance, astronomy, statistical physics, particle physics, chemistry, and nuclear physics. Documented in 48 preprints (2026), all permanently archived on Zenodo with full open data and code, and indexed on SSRN. The system has achieved paradigm demotion of foundational axioms across six disciplines: universality (statistical physics), gauge field theory (particle physics), periodic law (chemistry), the nuclear shell model (nuclear physics), the standard planetary-radius assumption (astronomy), and interest rate parity (finance).
我的研究构建了一套科学发现操作系统——一个程序化的、跨学科普适的、非人格化的框架，用于从客观数据中提取可靠规律。该系统包含四个组件：信息同构定律（认识论根基）、因子层级定律（本体论：规则因子与执行因子，三条公理）、可检验性原则（认识论标准：超越可证伪性）和可检验性规范（方法论：六个功能维度，功能稳定工具可变）。已在六大独立学科中部署——金融学、天文学、统计物理、粒子物理、化学与核物理。成果记录于48篇预印本（2026），全部永久存档于Zenodo并附带完整开放数据和代码，同时收录于SSRN。该操作系统已在六个学科中完成了对基础公理的范式降维：普适性（统计物理）、规范场论（粒子物理）、元素周期律（化学）、核壳层模型（核物理）、标准行星半径假设（天文学）、利率平价（金融学）。
