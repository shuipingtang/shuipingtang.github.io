### The Discovery of Factor Hierarchy

The Factor Hierarchy Law emerged from a cross-market anomaly: the **China-U.S. yield spread** was a **dual-identity factor** in gold, crude oil, bonds, FX, and capital flows — with high direct explanatory power *and* significant interaction effects. But when tested in **15 global equity markets** (Tang 2026e), it became a **pure regime factor**: direct R² ≈ 0, yet its interaction R² was 1.78× the direct R². The same variable played different roles in different markets. This cross-market variation revealed that factors are not inherently "important" or "unimportant" — their function depends on the market regime. From this insight emerged the Factor Hierarchy Law.
